Do not add channels just to fill 50 profiles. Keep stored browser sessions separate, but assign each active account to a real authorized owner and a defined platform role. The present worker's managed-profile adapter is Reddit-only; other platforms need their own discovery, policy and publishing handling.

## Discovery that finds needs rather than mentions

Use a community's newest-post view plus a rotating, bounded search for three problem families:

1. Beginner builders: `build app`, `no-code`, `MVP`, `which builder`, `cannot code`.
2. Business tasks: `client portal`, `booking app`, `internal tool`, `replace spreadsheet`, `enquiry tracker`.
3. Small websites: `portfolio`, `case-study pages`, `waitlist`, `landing page`.

Keywords nominate candidates only. Read the original question and author updates, then require an unresolved need, evidence of a relevant MeDo capability, and a place where product recommendations are welcome. Skip tutorials, self-promotion, solved requests, existing-tool debugging, repeated MeDo recommendations, and unverified mandatory requirements. Read recent replies for duplication; don't mistake another commenter's requirements for the original author's.

Use stable thread IDs, incremental checkpoints, explicit coverage caps and backoff when a scan finds nothing. Never claim coverage of “all new posts” from a capped browser scan. A login, CAPTCHA, access denial or rate limit requires attention rather than profile rotation.

## Reply quality and pacing

Drafts should name MeDo directly when it fits, mention two details from the actual request, suggest one concrete first step and state one verified capability. Keep the language short and ordinary, without invented personal experience, forced slang, guarantees or a list of unrelated features. Include the actual team affiliation. A human reviews before publication.

The current configured limits are shared across the Reddit worker's profiles: at most 3 replies in a rolling 24 hours; at least 2 hours between replies; at most 2 per community/day with at least 6 hours between them; no repeated pitch to the same author for 7 days. These are ceilings, not targets or evidence of platform permission. Do not multiply them by 50 profiles. Future platform adapters must use a shared campaign ledger before enabling publication.

Keep discovery cadence and publication cadence separate. Drafting may be fast; reviewed posting waits in a queue. Artificial mouse movements or deliberate typing mistakes add no content value and do not establish permission or prevent spam.

## Channels deferred

- [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/newsguidelines.html): potential human-written Show HN experiment; no generated/AI-edited comments or automated posting.
- [Bubble Forum](https://forum.bubble.io/guidelines): not a MeDo reply target; its rules explicitly prohibit derailing discussions into competing products or participating solely to migrate users off Bubble.
- [No Code Founders](https://www.nocodefounders.com/list-your-tool): currently a paid listing, so excluded from the no-purchase setup.
- [NoCodeDevs](https://www.nocodedevs.com/): now redirects to MakerThrive's distribution/partnership site; do not rely on old claims of an open free discussion community.

Measure qualified visits, registrations and activation per channel, alongside model tokens and review minutes. Use those results to choose the next adapter. Post count, profile count and upvotes are not the success metric.
